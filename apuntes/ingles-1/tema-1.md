# Tema 1 — Verbo TO BE (presente)

## 1. Formas según el sujeto

| Sujeto | Forma larga | Forma corta |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | 'm |
| He / She / It | is | 's |
| You / We / They | are | 're |

El verbo TO BE equivale a "ser" o "estar" en español. Es la base para identificar, describir y ubicar.

## 2. Usos específicos

- Identificación: nombrar personas u objetos. Ej.: She is Lorena.
- Origen: be + from + country. Ej.: I am from the USA.
- Profesiones: Martin is a lawyer.
- Edad: They are 20 years old.
- Ubicación de objetos: The Eiffel Tower is in Paris.
- Precios: The camera is 200$.

## 3. Estructura negativa

Sujeto + am not / isn't / aren't.

- I am not...
- He / She / It isn't...
- You / We / They aren't...

## 4. Estructura interrogativa (Yes/No)

Verb Be + Sujeto + Complemento?

- Are you a student?
- Respuestas: Yes, I am. / No, I am not.

## 5. Contracciones

### Afirmativas (sujeto + verbo)

I am → I'm; You are → You're; He is → He's; She is → She's; It is → It's; We are → We're; They are → They're.

Ejemplos:

- He is in the house. / He's in the house.
- Juan is in the house. / Juan's in the house.
- The car is in the garage. / The car's in the garage.

Aclaración: con preposición de lugar, Juan's significa "Juan está". Se diferencia del posesivo porque la frase tiene la estructura de una oración completa con el verbo.

Excepción: si el nombre termina en "s" (Carlos, Doris), no se usa la forma corta. Se mantiene la forma larga: Carlos is in the house.

### Negativas

Opción 1 (la más usada con "I"): Sujeto + contracción del verbo + not.

- I'm not, You're not, He's not, She's not, It's not, We're not, They're not.

Opción 2: Sujeto + contracción del verbo con not.

- You aren't, He isn't, She isn't, It isn't, We aren't, They aren't. (Con "I", "I am not" no lleva contracción simultánea.)

Ejemplo: He is not in the house. / He's not in the house. / He isn't in the house.

## 6. Ejercicios de práctica (contracciones)

Afirmativo (forma larga → forma corta):

- You are playing soccer. → You're playing soccer.
- They are studying in the library. → They're studying in the library.
- We are eating pizza. → We're eating pizza.
- She is reading a book. → She's reading a book.

Negativo (contracción del verbo + not):

- He isn't sleeping.
- We aren't dancing.
- You aren't cooking in the kitchen.
- They aren't using their car.
- It isn't raining.
