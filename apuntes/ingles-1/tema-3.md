# Tema 3 — Presente Continuo

## 1. Uso

Acciones en progreso en el momento de hablar. Expresiones de tiempo: now, right now, at the moment, at present.

## 2. Estructura

Afirmativa: Subject + to be + verb-ing.

- I am working / I'm working.
- He / She / It is working / He's / She's working.
- We / You / They are working / We're / They're working.

Negativa: Subject + to be + not + verb-ing.

- I am not working / I'm not working.
- He / She / It is not working / isn't working.
- We / You / They are not working / aren't working.

Interrogativa: to be + Subject + verb-ing?

- Am I working?
- Is he / she / it working?
- Are we / you / they working?

Con pregunta WH: What / Where / Why + to be + subject + verb-ing?

- What is Peter doing? — He is working on a project at the moment.

## 3. Reglas de -ING

- La terminación -ING solo se aplica a verbos.
- Verbo terminado en -e: se suprime la e y se agrega -ing. Ej.: believe → believing; practice → practicing.
- Verbos monosilábicos CVC (consonante-vocal-consonante): se duplica la última consonante y se agrega -ing. Ej.: jog → jogging; chop → chopping.
- Excepción CVC: no se duplica si termina en W, X, Y o Z. Ej.: draw → drawing; play → playing.

## 4. Presente Continuo vs. Presente Simple

- Presente simple: rutinas, hábitos, verdades generales. Expresiones: always, sometimes, never, every day, twice a week.
- Presente Continuo: acción ocurriendo ahora mismo. Expresiones: now, right now, at the moment.
- Regla clave: Simple = frecuencia / hábito; Continuous = este instante.

Ejemplos laborales (Presente Continuo):

- They are having a meeting in the conference room.
- She is answering the phone right now.
- The boss is talking to a client on the phone.
- He is checking inventory in the storeroom.
- They are fixing the printer.
- Lucas and Mark are writing reports at their desks.
- He is giving a presentation to the team.
